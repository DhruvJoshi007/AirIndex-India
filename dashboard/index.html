Method. Elementary aggregate = route × booking window. Elementary index = Jevons (geometric mean of all clean fares that day ÷ geometric mean in the base period). Routes and windows are combined with fixed weights, as in CPI. Missing cells carry forward. Monthly value = mean of daily values.

  
Demo data. Fares on this page come from simulated portals that mimic dynamic pricing, seasonality and messy page text. No live website was scraped.
